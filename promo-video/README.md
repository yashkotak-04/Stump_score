# StumpScore Promo Video Production Package 🎬🏏

Welcome to the official video production kit for **StumpScore** (live web app: [stumpscore.vercel.app](https://stumpscore.vercel.app)).

This directory contains everything needed to script, generate, voice, edit, and publish a high-converting 30–35 second promotional video across **Instagram Reels**, **YouTube Shorts**, and **TikTok**.

## 🚀 Quick Start Production Workflow

1. **Step 1: Voiceover Generation**
   * Copy the script text from [`voiceover-and-audio.md`](./voiceover-and-audio.md).
   * Open [ElevenLabs](https://elevenlabs.io/) or your preferred recording studio.
   * Generate or record with the energetic commercial sports voice (e.g. *Adam* or *Antoni*).
2. **Step 2: AI B-Roll & Footage Capture**
   * Paste the prompts from [`ai-video-prompts.md`](./ai-video-prompts.md) into Runway Gen-3 Alpha or Kling AI to produce cinematic stadium and gameplay clips.
   * Screen record the live web app at [stumpscore.vercel.app](https://stumpscore.vercel.app) (Score simulator, StumpAI Guru window, Master Story Studio).
3. **Step 3: Post-Production Editing**
   * Open your editor (Premiere Pro, DaVinci Resolve, or CapCut).
   * Set sequence to **1080 x 1920 (9:16 Vertical, 60 FPS)**.
   * Follow the editing guide in [`edit-guide.md`](./edit-guide.md) to sync cuts with the BGM and apply the SFX cues.
4. **Step 4: Publish & Launch**
   * Export in H.264 high-bitrate MP4.
   * Copy the caption, call-to-action link, and hashtags from [`caption-and-hashtags.md`](./caption-and-hashtags.md).
   * Post to Instagram Reels ([@stump_score](https://instagram.com/stump_score)), YouTube Shorts, and TikTok.

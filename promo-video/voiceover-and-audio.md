# StumpScore Promo Video: Voiceover & Audio Design Guide

---

## 1. Voiceover (VO) Profile & Casting
* **Tone:** Energetic, confident, authentic cricket enthusiast, authoritative yet friendly.
* **Pacing:** Fast conversational pace (~145–160 words per minute) with sharp micro-pauses for impact.
* **Target Accent:** Warm Neutral Global English or Modern Indo-Global Cricket Commentator style (reminiscent of Harsha Bhogle / Danny Morrison energy).

### Recommended AI Voice Generator Setup (ElevenLabs)
* **Recommended Voices:**
  * **Option A:** "Adam" (Deep, confident, engaging narrative) — Stability: 45%, Clarity: 85%, Style: 25%
  * **Option B:** "Antoni" (Energetic commercial announcer) — Stability: 50%, Clarity: 80%
  * **Option C (Indian Sports Voice):** "Aryan" / "Kabir" (Authentic subcontinent cricket tournament flavor)
* **Total Script Word Count:** 118 Words (~33 Seconds at normal cadence).

## 3. Background Music (BGM) Specification

* **Genre:** Hybrid Sports Trap / Cyber-Electronic Hip-Hop with orchestral brass accents.
* **Tempo:** 130 – 134 BPM (Matches high-energy scrolling and boundary highlights).
* **Key:** D Minor or F Minor (Dynamic, driving, athletic).
* **Structure:**
  * **00:00 – 00:04:** Filtered low-pass intro beat (building tension under the paper scoresheet).
  * **00:05:** Hard 808 bass drop + brass hit as StumpScore app is revealed.
  * **00:05 – 00:27:** Driving rhythm with rhythmic hi-hat rolls and melodic synthesizer arpeggios.
  * **00:28 – 00:35:** Final drum roll buildup resolving into a sustained triumphant stadium chord.
* **Reference Tracks:**
  * *The Champion* style sports promo beat (Epidemic Sound / Artlist: search tags `#cricket #sports_promo #energetic_trap`).
